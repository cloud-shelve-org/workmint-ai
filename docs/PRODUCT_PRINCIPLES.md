# Product principles

## North star
Help people complete meaningful tasks faster or more reliably with focused AI utilities.

## What qualifies as a utility?
A utility must have a clearly defined user, input, output, and successful outcome. “Chat with AI” alone is not a product proposition.

## Prioritization rubric
Score candidate tools on problem frequency, pain intensity, willingness to pay, discoverability, implementation effort, AI inference cost, privacy/compliance risk, and defensibility. Validate demand before building a large suite.

## Launch standard
Every public tool needs clear instructions, examples, transparent limitations, privacy disclosures, abuse controls, usage-cost monitoring, and a measurable success event.
